# Equivariant Neural Wavefunctions for Hydride Superconductors

## Abstract
An E(3)-equivariant neural quantum state predicts a 287 K transition in metastable LaH10-xNx at 182 GPa while reducing variational energy error to 2.4 meV.

## Methods
Equivariant message passing parameterizes orbitals and a long-range Jastrow factor. Stochastic reconfiguration minimizes energy over 65,536 walkers with twist averaging and finite-size extrapolation.

## Results
The method reaches 287 K predicted Tc and 8.1x sampling throughput. Five independent chains and blocked bootstrap intervals quantify Monte Carlo uncertainty.

## Limitations
The phase is metastable and requires experimental synthesis. Pseudopotential and finite-size biases remain non-zero.

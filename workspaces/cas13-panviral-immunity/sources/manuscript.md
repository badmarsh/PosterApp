# EvoGuide: Structure-Aware Cas13 Pan-Viral Immunity

## Abstract
EvoGuide designs four-guide Cas13d ensembles that achieve 99.2% viral RNA knockdown with no detectable escape across 48 serial cultures.

## Methods
A pangenome transformer scores conservation, RNA accessibility, collateral risk, and mutational escape. Submodular selection enforces manufacturability and off-target constraints.

## Results
EvoGuide improved knockdown from 91.6% for a single guide to 99.2%; escape fell from 11 of 48 cultures to zero of 48. Endpoints were assessed blinded.

## Limitations
The viral panel cannot represent all future variants. Delivery, immunogenicity, and in-vivo durability require prospective evaluation.
